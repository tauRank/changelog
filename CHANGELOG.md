# tauRank changelog

Product updates for [tauRank](https://taurank.com), the Airbnb and Booking.com rank tracker that searches from
the countries your guests come from. Newest first; each entry is also a release in this repo's
[Releases](https://github.com/tauRank/changelog/releases). The canonical copy is [taurank.com/changelog](https://taurank.com/changelog).

## 2026-10-27 · Your ranking for one stay, and the days something changed

- The position chart can follow one stay: pick a stay under **Open dates** and the chart shows where that search placed you, day by day, in each country. **Overall** is still the average across every search we run for you.
- Flags above the chart mark the days your rating or review count changed, and, when you are looking at one stay, the days your price for those nights changed. Hover a flag to see what it changed from.
- A scan that did not find your listing is a point at the bottom of the line, and the line runs through it, so a drop out of the results reads as a drop.
- **Where you rank for your dates** shows a country that searched a stay and did not find you as a dot at the bottom, instead of leaving the stay looking unscanned.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-27)

## 2026-10-26 · Checks start on your open dates, and a menu that fits a phone

- A [one-time check](https://taurank.com/check) now starts on your listing's first open dates, the stay you are trying to sell, rather than a weekend that may already be booked. Each country's result says its market, stay and party size.
- Prices on the check page and in the credit packs now say "+ VAT", and the button says what it spends: one credit.
- Each plan's button sits right under its price on [pricing](https://taurank.com/pricing), as well as at the foot of the card.
- The [demo](https://taurank.com/demo) page shows the demo's own dashboard beside the form.
- On a phone, the site's menu folds behind a button instead of running off the edge. Signed in, Sign out sits next to Dashboard.
- The position chart is simpler: a scan that did not find your listing is one dot at the bottom, and the legend only names what the chart does not already show.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-26)

## 2026-10-25 · Sign in with Google, and an email when your results are in

- Sign in with your Google account, or with your email as before. Signing in opens over the page you are on, so you stay where you were.
- Buying a check, a credit pack or a plan now starts by signing in, so every purchase lands on an account you can open again.
- We email you once your results are ready: a check, the first reading of a new listing, or a refresh. You can close the page while it runs.
- A one-time check reads every results page, so it can always say where your listing stands, however far down it is.
- Check the same listing on two different days and it gets its own position chart.
- Party sizes go up to 16 guests, and new listings default to 3-night stays.
- A Booking.com property with several room types now shows how many it has, and how many guests the largest one sleeps.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-25)

## 2026-10-23 · Check a listing from the menu, and a demo link that lasts a day

- [Check a listing](https://taurank.com/check) is in the top menu. The page starts with the form, shows a real answer as an example, and says how long one takes: about three minutes.
- If we cannot open the listing you checked - removed, private, or not a listing link - nothing is searched and the credit goes straight back to your balance.
- The [demo](https://taurank.com/demo) page shows the demo's real dashboard, names the listing it tracks, and says what happens after you enter your email.
- The demo link we email you now works for 24 hours, on any device: ask for it on your phone, open it later on your laptop.
- Cookie choices: allow analytics and marketing cookies separately, both, or neither, and change your mind any time under **Cookie settings** in the footer. Because the choices changed, you are asked once more.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-23)

## 2026-10-21 · More in Starter, and buying without an account first

- Starter now searches from three countries instead of two, and includes open dates on every listing: 60 days ahead, up to two stays. See [pricing](https://taurank.com/pricing).
- Credit packs and plans can be bought without making an account first: pay, and you come back signed in. If the email you paid with already has a tauRank account, we email it a sign-in link instead.
- Choosing a search window your plan does not include now offers the add-on that unlocks it, or Pro, right there.
- Two new articles on the [blog](https://taurank.com/blog): [Does Airbnb show different prices by country?](https://taurank.com/blog/does-airbnb-show-different-prices-by-country), from 1,764 first pages, and [Which guests does Airbnb treat as price-sensitive?](https://taurank.com/blog/which-countries-are-price-sensitive), across seven countries.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-21)

## 2026-10-16 · Search as a local guest would

- A listing can now be searched the way a local guest searches: in each country's own language, and on Airbnb's site for that country, such as airbnb.de, instead of in English on airbnb.com and booking.com. Tick **Search like a local guest** when you add a listing or on its edit page. Prices stay in your city's currency, so the two compare.
- You can track the same listing both ways at once, to see whether local guests rank it differently. The local copy appears on your list as its own listing, with its countries marked **local**, and counts as a listing on your plan.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-16)

## 2026-10-12 · The dashboard on a phone, a FAQ and two guides

The listing screen now works on a phone, panel by panel, instead of shrinking the desktop layout.

- **Open dates** become a list of the stays we searched, with the price per night and your position from each country, and your booked dates in between.
- **Position over time** draws one short chart per country.
- **Price against position** becomes four counts - cheaper or pricier, ranking above or below you - and names the listings that are cheaper and rank above you.
- A [FAQ](https://taurank.com/#faq) on the home page.
- A guide: [Why did my Airbnb ranking drop?](https://taurank.com/why-did-my-airbnb-ranking-drop), four checks in order before you change your listing.
- [tauRank vs Rankbreeze](https://taurank.com/taurank-vs-rankbreeze): where each tool fits.

[Read on taurank.com](https://taurank.com/changelog#2026-10-12)
